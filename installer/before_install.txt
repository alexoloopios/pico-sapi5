Pico SAPI5
==========

This installs six text-to-speech voices built on the SVOX Pico engine, and makes
them available to any application on this computer that uses SAPI 5:

    Pico American English      Pico Spanish
    Pico British English       Pico French
    Pico German                Pico Italian

Both a 32-bit and a 64-bit engine are installed, so applications of either kind
can use the voices. They share one copy of the speech data.

What to expect
--------------

The voices speak at 16 kHz. They are small, fast and highly intelligible, which
is what they were designed for; they are not intended to sound like a recording
of a person.

Rate, pitch and volume all work as they do with any other SAPI 5 voice, and the
engine reports word and sentence positions as it speaks, so screen readers can
follow along and highlight what is being said.

Notes
-----

Administrative rights are required. SAPI only looks for voices in the
machine-wide part of the registry, so there is no per-user installation.

Applications that are already running, screen readers included, usually need to
be restarted before a newly installed voice shows up in their voice list.
